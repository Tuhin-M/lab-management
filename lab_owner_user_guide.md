# Lab Owner User Guide: Onboarding & Managing Tests

Welcome to the Ekitsa Lab Management platform! This guide is designed for Lab Owners and Administrative Staff. It will walk you through the step-by-step process of adding your laboratory to the platform and setting up the diagnostic tests you offer.

No technical knowledge is required—just follow the steps below as you navigate through the application.

---

## Part 1: Onboarding Your Laboratory

Before you can offer services, you need to register your laboratory's details so patients can find you, see your facilities, and book appointments.

### Preparation Checklist
Before you start, please have the following information handy:
- **Basic Details**: Lab Name, Type (e.g., Diagnostic, Pathology), and your official Registration Number.
- **Contact Info**: Business Email, Primary Phone Number, and Website (optional).
- **Location**: Full street address, City, State, and ZIP code.
- **Media**: A high-quality logo and photos of your facility.
- **Certifications**: Details of any accreditations (e.g., NABL, ISO).

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Log In to Your Dashboard**
   - Go to the login page and enter your credentials. Ensure your account is set up with "Lab Owner" access.
   - Upon logging in, you will be directed to the **Lab Owner Dashboard**.

2. **Start the Onboarding Process**
   - On your dashboard, look for the **"Add Lab"** button (usually represented by a "+" icon) and click it. 
   - *Note: Depending on where you click, you might see a quick 2-step popup window or be taken to a full-page detailed form.*

3. **Fill in the Basic Information**
   - **Lab Name**: Enter the official name of your lab.
   - **Lab Type**: Select the category that best describes your facility from the dropdown menu.
   - **Registration**: Input your official medical registration number.

4. **Provide Location and Contact Details**
   - Enter your full address so patients can easily locate you on maps.
   - Add your public-facing email and phone number.

5. **Upload Images & Certifications (Full Form)**
   - Upload your lab's logo.
   - Add photos of your facility to build trust with patients.
   - Check the boxes for any special facilities you offer (e.g., Home Collection, 24/7 Emergency, Wheelchair Accessible).

6. **Submit and Complete**
   - Review your details and click the **"Complete Onboarding"** or **"Submit"** button.
   - You will see a green success message confirming your lab has been added. Your new lab will now appear as a card on your dashboard!

---

## Part 2: Adding Tests to Your Lab

Now that your lab is active on the platform, you need to tell the system which diagnostic tests you offer, how much they cost, and how long they take.

### Preparation Checklist
For each test you want to add, you will need:
- **Test Details**: Name (e.g., "Complete Blood Count") and Category (e.g., "Hematology").
- **Sample Type**: E.g., Blood, Urine, Saliva.
- **Pricing**: The standard price and any discount percentage you wish to offer.
- **Logistics**: Turnaround time (how long until results are ready) and test duration.
- **Parameters**: The specific things the test checks (e.g., Hemoglobin, WBC Count).

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Select Your Lab**
   - From the Lab Owner Dashboard, click on the specific Lab you just created. This takes you to the **Lab Details Page**.

2. **Navigate to "Add Test"**
   - Look for the **"Add Test"** button and click it. This will open the Test Configuration Form.

3. **Fill in the Test Basics**
   - **Test Name**: Type the name clearly (e.g., "Lipid Profile").
   - **Category**: Group your test logically (e.g., "Biochemistry").
   - **Sample Type**: Select the required biological sample from the dropdown menu.

4. **Set Pricing and Timing**
   - **Price**: Enter your base price.
   - **Discount (Optional)**: If you are offering a promotion, enter the percentage discount. The system will automatically calculate the final price for patients.
   - **Turnaround Time & Duration**: Let patients know exactly when to expect their reports (e.g., "24 Hours").

5. **Define Test Parameters**
   - Scroll down to the "Parameters" section.
   - Enter the specific markers this test looks for. 
   - Click the **"Add Parameter"** button to add more fields if the test checks multiple things (e.g., for a Lipid Profile, you would add Cholesterol, Triglycerides, HDL, LDL as separate parameters).

6. **Save Your Test**
   - Double-check your entries and click **"Save & Add Test"**.
   - A success message will appear, and you will be returned to your Lab Details page.

7. **Manage Your Tests**
   - On the Lab Details page, you can view your test catalog.
   - You can easily toggle tests **"Active"** or **"Inactive"** using the switch next to each test if you temporarily run out of supplies or stop offering a specific service.

---

> [!TIP]
> **Need to make changes later?** 
> You can always return to your dashboard, click on your lab, and edit your details or manage your active tests at any time. Patient bookings will only be allowed for tests marked as "Active".
